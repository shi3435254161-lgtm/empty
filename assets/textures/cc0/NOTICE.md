## ambientCG

Source: https://ambientcg.com
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (https://ambientcg.com/license)

Bundled assets:
- acg_tiles107 (Tiles107, 2K-JPG)
- acg_tiles036 (Tiles036, 2K-JPG)
- acg_marble021 (Marble021, 2K-JPG)
- acg_metal009 (Metal009, 1K-JPG)
- acg_wood049 (Wood049, 1K-JPG)

`ambientcg-assets.json` records the source URL, archive SHA-256, map SHA-256,
resolution, and normalized filenames for every ambientCG material.

These textures are used as high fidelity PBR material inputs for the cabinet designer's wall, floor, countertop, wood and metal surfaces.
